Revenue Growth Case Study

We Increased Conversion by 24% for a $1.2M Shopify Store.

When a store is already doing seven figures, "guessing" isn't an option. We audited 10,000+ user sessions to identify the exact friction points stopping the checkout.

The Scaling Ceiling

The client had hit a plateau. Despite increasing ad spend, their Conversion Rate (CR) stayed stuck at 1.8%. They were losing thousands of dollars every month to a "leaky" funnel.

● High Bounce on PDP

Users couldn't find the "Size Guide" or "Shipping Info" quickly, leading to abandonment.

● Multi-Step Checkout Friction

Too many fields and no "Express Checkout" options on mobile devices.

The UX-First Execution

1

Visual Hierarchy Overhaul

We used **React** to build a custom Product Page layout that prioritizes the "Add to Cart" and "Value Propositions" above the fold on all devices.

2

Micro-Interaction Polish

Implemented instant feedback on cart additions and smooth transitions using **CSS3 and JavaScript**, reducing the perceived wait time for the user.

3

Trust Signal Injection

Integrated dynamic social proof and secure payment badges at high-friction points (Cart & Checkout) to boost buyer confidence.
