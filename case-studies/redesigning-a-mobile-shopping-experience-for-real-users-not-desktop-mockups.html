Redesigning a Mobile Experience for Real Users, Not Desktop Mockups.

Most agencies design on 27-inch monitors. At Olab, we design for the thumb. We rebuilt a Shopify fashion brand's mobile site from the ground up to solve "Small Screen Friction."

"The Desktop-Preview Trap"

The client’s previous store was a "responsive" desktop site. It looked great on a laptop, but on a phone, buttons were too small, the "Add to Cart" was buried, and the product images took forever to load.

Pain Points Found

  • • Users had to scroll 3 screens to find the price.
  • • "Buy Now" button was outside the natural "Thumb Zone."
  • • Heavy JavaScript caused 4-second interaction delays.
01.

The Thumb Zone

We moved all critical interactions (Buy, Filter, Search) to the bottom 30% of the screen.

02.

Skeleton Loading

Implemented React-based skeleton screens so the site feels "instant" even on 4G connections.

03.

Native Feel

Used CSS-driven horizontal swiping for product galleries—no clunky desktop arrows.

04.

Express Pay

Prioritized Apple Pay & ShopPay at the top of the mobile checkout flow.
